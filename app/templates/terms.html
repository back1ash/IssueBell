Terms

By using IssueBell, you agree to use it lawfully and in a way that respects GitHub projects, maintainers, and other contributors.

Responsible contribution

An alert is an invitation to review an issue, not ownership of it. Read each project's contribution guide, avoid low-effort or automated spam, and follow maintainer instructions before starting work.

Service availability

IssueBell is provided on an as-is and best-effort basis. Delivery may be delayed or unavailable because of GitHub, Discord, network, rate-limit, or maintenance events. Do not rely on IssueBell for emergencies or safety-critical monitoring.

Accounts

You are responsible for the connected Discord and GitHub accounts. You may disconnect GitHub or delete your IssueBell account at any time. Access may be limited when necessary to protect the service or other users.
